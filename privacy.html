Privacy Policy

The short version

K8 runs entirely on your computer and collects nothing. When you buy, we keep just enough to get your key to you: your order number, your key and your email address. That's it.

Who we are

LilBuba.ai is run by Joanna Browning, a sole trader in England, who decides how this data is used (the "controller"). For anything about your data, email support@lilbuba.ai.

What K8 does

K8 scans files on your local machine to find duplicates and unnecessary files. All scanning, analysis, and file operations happen locally. K8 does not connect to the internet during scanning. No file data, metadata, filenames, folder structures, or scan results are transmitted anywhere. Ever.

What we collect

K8 itself collects nothing. Zero telemetry. Zero analytics. Zero tracking. We don't know how many files you scan, how often you use K8, or what's on your computer.

The data involved in your K8 purchase is:

  • Payment information — handled entirely by our payment processor, Paddle. We never see or store your card details. Paddle acts as Merchant of Record and processes payments in accordance with their own privacy policy.
  • Licence key: our key service makes your key when you buy and shows it to you on the download page. In K8, the key is stored on your own machine in a settings file, and K8 never sends it anywhere.
  • Your order and your email address: when you buy, Paddle tells our key service (it runs on Cloudflare) about your order, and the download page sends it your order number, so it can check the order with Paddle and give you your key. The key service gets from Paddle the email address you gave at checkout and keeps it with your key and order number, so we can email you a copy of your key and help if you ever lose it.
  • Email address: apart from the copy kept with your key (above), only if you contact us at support@lilbuba.ai or sign up for product updates. We use it solely to respond to your enquiry or send product updates you requested. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Why we use it, and the law that lets us

  • Your key, order number and email: to give you your key, email it to you and help if you lose it. We need this to provide your licence (contract).
  • Emails you send us: to answer you (our legitimate interest in helping you).
  • Product-update emails: only if you ask for them, and you can stop them any time (your consent).
  • Website visit data (your IP address and browser details): to run the website, keep it secure and count visits (our legitimate interest in running a safe, working site).

You don't have to give us anything to download K8 or to scan. To buy, Paddle needs your email address; without it we can't send you your key.

How long we keep it

We keep your key, order number and email address for as long as we sell and support K8, so we can always resend your key. Ask us and we'll delete them sooner (keep a copy of your key if you do). We delete support emails once they're no longer needed, and product-update emails stop as soon as you ask.

Cookies

K8 itself uses no cookies. This website sets no cookies of its own. It keeps a few small notes in your own browser so the site works, such as which Lil Buba you picked and, while you're buying, your order number so the download page can fetch your key. They aren't used to track you. Our product pages load Paddle's checkout script as soon as they open, and that script also loads ProfitWell, a Paddle service. Paddle may use its own cookies for these: see Paddle's privacy policy.

Third parties

We never sell your personal data. These services handle some data when you visit this website or buy K8, each under its own privacy policy:

  • Paddle: sells K8 to you as our reseller and Merchant of Record, takes payment and handles tax and refunds. Its checkout script, and its ProfitWell script, load on our product pages.
  • Google (Gmail): we send and receive email through Google, including the email with your key.
  • Cloudflare: hosts this website and our key service, and counts page visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. Like any web host, it sees your IP address and browser details.
  • Google Fonts: the website's fonts load from Google, which sees your IP address when they load.
  • Our download host: the K8 installer downloads from a file-hosting service run by Microsoft, which sees your IP address when you download it.

K8 itself, the app on your PC, contacts none of them.

Outside the UK

Some of these services store or handle data outside the UK, mainly in the US. Each uses the safeguards UK law requires for that, as set out in its own privacy terms.

Your rights (UK GDPR)

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to limit or object to how we use it, or to have it sent to you in a usable format. Where we rely on your consent (product-update emails), you can withdraw it any time. Email support@lilbuba.ai and we'll reply within a month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, tell us first and we'll acknowledge your complaint within 30 days. You can also complain to the UK data protection regulator, the Information Commission (formerly the ICO), at ico.org.uk.

Children

K8 is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it here with a new date.
