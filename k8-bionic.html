K8 Bionic: the questions we get asked

What is K8 Bionic? +
K8 Bionic is the duplicate file and photo finder for ordinary life: the Downloads folder nobody opens, the eleven nearly-identical shots of the same sunset, the backup of the backup of the backup. It finds exact duplicates and near-identical photos, shows you what it found, and never removes anything on its own. Anything you do remove goes to a 30-day quarantine you can restore from with one click. It runs entirely on your own machine. £24.95, one-time, lifetime licence.
How does K8 find duplicates so fast? +
Most duplicate finders end up comparing every file against every other, which is why they crawl on big drives. K8's patent-pending approach rules most files out before any comparing starts. On a real library of 3,318 photos it needed 6,840 checks instead of 5,496,270, which is 99.88% fewer. It's built for very large drives, and we'll publish measured scan times as we benchmark them.
Will it find similar photos, not just exact copies? +
Yes. Exact copies are the easy part. K8 Bionic also looks for near-identical photos, because we all take loads of shots hoping one comes out perfect. It groups the near-duplicates together so you can pick the keeper and let the rest go. You review every group, and nothing moves until you tick it.
What is Source of Truth mode? +
It's for when you already know where the good copy lives. You set a master folder, such as your proper photo library or your organised music folder, and K8 Bionic looks for duplicates of it everywhere else. Originals in the master folder are always safe. It's the tidiest way to clear out the copies that sprawled into Downloads, the Desktop and half a dozen old backup folders.
How does K8 handle OneDrive cloud files? +
K8 detects cloud-only files (the cloud icon in Explorer) and skips them automatically. No accidental mass downloads, no freezing, no bandwidth surprises. Files that are fully synced locally are scanned normally.
Will K8 permanently delete my files? +
No. All removals go through a 30-day quarantine system first. Files sit there fully intact and restorable with one click. Even after 30 days, K8 asks for confirmation before permanent deletion. We've all heard the horror stories about tools that nuked someone's wedding photos. That will never happen with K8.
Does K8 send my data anywhere? +
No. Absolutely not. K8 runs entirely on your local machine. It does not connect to the internet during scanning. Zero telemetry. Zero analytics. Zero tracking. We don't know how often you run it, how many files you scan, or whether you prefer dark mode. Your privacy is not our product.
Do I need to create an account? +
No. K8 has no accounts, no login, no registration. You download it, enter your licence key, and you're done. We don't want your email address, your usage data, or your firstborn. Just clean up your files in peace.
